About me

Ing. Ph.D. · est. 1989

I'm a researcher at the Czech Technical University in Prague, working on cementitious materials and the instrumentation around them. My favourite spot is the gap between two worlds: on one side concrete, formwork and construction sites; on the other, microcontrollers, PCBs and machine learning.

I studied civil engineering in Prague, then spent three years in Yokohama, Japan, earning a Ph.D. on strain-hardening cementitious composites. Since coming back to CTU I design and build measurement devices — pressure chambers, monitoring electronics, NB-IoT sensor nodes with on-device AI — and use them to generate research data that helps engineers make faster, safer, more economical decisions.

I also teach, which is the other reason this site exists — students, your Student Zone is one click away.
